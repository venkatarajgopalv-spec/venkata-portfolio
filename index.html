01 — index.about

About Me

More than a degree — a journey of growth

about.jpg

My professional journey began with a Bachelor's degree in Mechanical Engineering, where I learned to think logically, solve problems, and approach challenges with discipline and precision.

As I explored different industries, I found myself drawn to the business side of organisations — how companies make strategic decisions, manage financial resources, lead teams, and adapt to changing environments. That curiosity led me to pursue an MBA: not simply to earn another qualification, but to build the perspective needed to create meaningful business impact.

Along the way I've worked on projects across corporate finance, accounting, and economics, gained exposure through internships, and stayed curious about governance, public policy, and current affairs — interests that shape how I understand the connections between business, society, and the economy.

Outside the classroom, I play cricket and badminton — sports that taught me teamwork, resilience, and how to perform under pressure. Today, I see myself as a lifelong learner, committed to a career where I can solve meaningful business problems and grow into a responsible, value-creating leader.

// philosophy.md

Growth is not defined by where we begin, but by our willingness to learn, adapt, and move forward. Every experience — success, challenge, or new opportunity — adds to the person we become. I aspire to build a career where curiosity inspires learning, integrity guides decisions, and leadership is measured by the positive impact created for others.

Learning Beyond the Classroom

Tarang Bazaar is our college's annual student business festival, where teams conceive a business idea, build it from the ground up, and run it live for a day in front of the campus — a real, hands-on entrepreneurship simulation rather than a classroom case study.

My team and I conceived, priced, and operated our own food-business venture end-to-end: sourcing, pricing, marketing, and live customer sales — giving me direct exposure to planning, teamwork, and the day-to-day realities of running a business under pressure.

I also helped organise and run the broader management business festival, and attended a Digital Marketing Workshop covering modern marketing strategy and digital customer engagement.
